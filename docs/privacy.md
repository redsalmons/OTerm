# OceanTerm Privacy Policy / OceanTerm 隐私政策

**English**

OceanTerm does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or usage data.

- All connection information, credentials, and settings are stored locally on your device.
- Passwords are stored in the system Keychain and never leave your device.
- File transfers and terminal sessions connect only to the SSH/SFTP servers you explicitly configure.
- OceanTerm contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

If you have questions, contact: https://github.com/redsalmons/OTerm/issues

**中文**

OceanTerm 不收集、不存储、不传输、不共享任何个人数据或使用数据。

- 所有连接信息、凭据和设置仅存储在你的设备本地。
- 密码保存在系统钥匙串中，不会离开你的设备。
- 文件传输与终端会话仅连接你明确配置的 SSH/SFTP 服务器。
- OceanTerm 不含任何分析、广告、追踪或第三方 SDK。

如有问题，请联系：https://github.com/redsalmons/OTerm/issues
